Buying behavior

How homeowners actually shop for a contractor

They find you on Google or a neighbor recommends you. Then they open three tabs. They look for photos of work like theirs, read reviews, and try to figure out whether you are a real crew or a guy with a truck. If your site is thin, they close the tab — you never even know you were in the running.

  • 01They shop in threes. You are always being compared.
  • 02Photos of your work do more selling than any paragraph.
  • 03Price is rarely first — reliability and clean work are.
  • 04The estimate request is the only conversion that matters.
What matters most

The features that actually move the needle

Not a feature list — the specific things that turn a visitor in contractors & construction into someone who reaches out.

01

Before & after galleries

Organized by project type, with big honest photography. The single highest-value asset on a contractor site.

02

Free estimate request

Short form, on every page, phone number tap-to-call in the header. No 12-field questionnaire.

03

Service area pages

The towns you actually work in, so you show up when someone searches "deck builder + your town."

04

Reviews front and center

Google reviews pulled onto the page near the estimate button, where they do the most work.

05

Financing & warranty

If you offer it, say it early. It removes the biggest objection on a $40k job.

06

Project portfolio detail

A few flagship jobs written up properly: scope, timeline, challenge, result.
