Our story

Rooted in family.
Roasted with purpose.

My future wife and I sat over a good cup of coffee and talked about the future — what would it hold and where would we go. It was by God's grace and direction that moved us to walk in faith and move from Southern CA, over 10 years ago, to the Central Coast.

Since then, we have been blessed beyond measure with four little ones — hence Tres Hermanos. We're more than a coffee brand; we're a family-built tradition rooted in quality, connection, and the simple joy of a really good cup.

Our goal is to make sure our coffee is consistent and of the highest quality and flavor. Our second goal is to progress and offer unique specialties that stand alone. And our third — that you enjoy our quaint footprint and come back again and again.

— The Tres Hermanos Family Templeton & Atascadero, California
Our freshly curated coffees

Two blends.
One family signature.

Our coffees are curated from our two in-house blends — Hacienda and Tres Hermanos Espresso. Unique to Tres Hermanos, they provide a well-balanced experience from our French Press to our specialty drinks, like the Tres Hermanos Latte.

The Hideaway

The Hideaway is exactly that — our coffee bar tucked inside Nana's Ice Cream just off El Camino Real. From the street it looks like an ice cream shop; step inside and you'll find the second Tres Hermanos. It's the everyday spot — order ahead, grab and go, or stick around for a scoop of Nana's on the side.

Same beans, same blends, same hands you'd find at the flagship — just served from a friendly counter shared with our favorite ice cream people in town.
