How it works

From site visit to files in days, not weeks.

  1. STEP 01 · DAY 0

    Scope the job

    Send the address, size and purpose. You get a fixed quote and a capture plan built around what your reviewer will want to see.

  2. STEP 02 · ON SITE

    Scan

    Most homes take 30 minutes up to an hour. Every room, the exterior, and the details drawings depend on.

  3. STEP 03 · 3–5 DAYS

    Deliver

    A shareable 3D model plus the plans, point cloud or CAD files you selected. Expedite delivers in 48 hours.

  4. STEP 04 · ONGOING

    Build from it

    Engineers, contractors and clients walk the same model remotely and measure what they need, before anyone drives to site.
