About

Paula Daoutis

Owner, Outsourced Administrative Services, LLC

Forty-five years of administrative work at the level where accuracy and discretion are not optional. Paula has supported chief executives, owners, and boards, and has served as an executive director — running an organization rather than only supporting one.

That perspective changes the work. She reads an agenda the way a director reads it, sees which vendor renewal will become a problem, and knows what a board actually needs in a packet.

Outsourced Administrative Services, LLC was built to make that experience available by the project or by the hour — to a two-person business as readily as to a large organization.
