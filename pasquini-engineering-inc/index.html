About Us

We Are Pasquini Engineering, Inc.

As a leading structural engineering firm in California, we offer full-service building design and engineering services, including civil and architectural solutions. Since 1990, Pasquini Engineering, Inc. has successfully designed and engineered thousands of residential projects for homeowners and commercial developments for general contractors, business owners, and investors.

Our mission is to provide efficient, professional service from project inception to completion. Our expert team brings decades of experience in structural engineering, building design, drafting, construction management, land development, and surveying. As one of the top California engineering firms, we have over 30 years of trusted service, making us your go-to partner for exceptional engineering solutions tailored to your needs.
