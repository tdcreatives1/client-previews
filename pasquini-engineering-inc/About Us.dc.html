Why Homeowners Call Us

Almost every project needs a permit. Every permit needs a licensed professional's stamp.

Whether it's an ADU, a second story, or a rebuild after a fire, your city or county will ask for plans signed by a licensed engineer or designer before work can start. Most homeowners discover this after they've already hired a contractor, then spend weeks chasing down three or four separate professionals.

Pasquini Engineering, Inc. puts all of them under one roof. Our designers, engineers, and surveyors work from the same set of drawings, so your plans are coordinated from the first sketch, and we value-engineer along the way to keep construction costs in check.
